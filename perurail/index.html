Case study

Testing PeruRail's live booking site

PeruRail sells train tickets to Machu Picchu. I tested its booking site the way I would test any system I don't own: explore it, rank the risks, design tests with formal techniques, automate what is safe to automate and report what I found. Everything ran on production, so nothing was booked and no personal data was entered.

How I worked
  1. 01ExploreWalked the booking flow by hand, read its rules and watched its network calls.
  2. 02Rank the risksScored what could go wrong for a traveller, so the riskiest areas were tested first.
  3. 03Design the testsBoundary values, decision tables, equivalence partitioning and use cases.
  4. 04Automate and reportPlaywright in Firefox and WebKit, with every defect written up for the business.

House rules for testing on production

  • Read-onlynever submits passenger data, never pays
  • One test at a timeone worker, runs never overlap
  • No evasionthe bot protection blocks Chromium, so Chromium is left out
  • Mock, don't hammerfailures are simulated in the browser with page.route()

    Pipeline and AI

    How it runs

    .github/workflows/playwright.yml
    1. Every push or pull requestruns the smoke tests, a quick check that keeps traffic to PeruRail low
    2. On demandruns the full suite and publishes both reports to GitHub Pages
    3. After every runGemini suggests why each failed test failed
    ai/ · Google Gemini
    • ai:triagesorts each failure into product defect, test defect, environment or flaky
    • ai:askanswers questions about PeruRail's policies and the suite, citing its sources
    • ai:i18nlists texts on the English pages that are not in English

    Advisory only: it suggests and a QA confirms. It never changes a test result, and the assistant found that the luggage policy contradicts itself (M6).

    Out of scope, on purpose
    • Passenger data and paymentwould create real reservations or hold real seats
    • Coupon codesguessing codes against production is not acceptable
    • Load and security testingnever against a production system I don't own
    • Chromiumthe bot protection denies it, and I respect that
